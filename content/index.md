## About

I'm Principal Architect, AI Engineering at [NetSPI](https://www.netspi.com/), where I design and build AI applications and the infrastructure underneath them.

I started in web application security consulting, spending a few years breaking into applications before deciding I'd rather build the systems myself.
Along the way I taught in NetSPI University, the program that trains NetSPI's associate pentesters, and wrote intentionally vulnerable apps for them to break.

Most of my work now is about measuring what AI systems can actually do.
I built [EchoBench](https://www.netspi.com/blog/technical-blog/ai-ml-pentesting/introducing-echobench-a-human-calibrated-benchmark-for-autonomous-pentesting/), a benchmark that scores autonomous pentesting agents against a measured cohort of human testers, and I write about AI security on the [NetSPI blog](https://www.netspi.com/authors/jake-scheetz/).

This site is for longer notes on fundamental AI research: how models learn, how we measure them, and where they break.
